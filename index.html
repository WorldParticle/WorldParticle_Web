02 / Pipeline

From local particles
to global context.

An explicit predictor handles known external forces. A learned corrector models particle interactions with local tokenization and compact global communication.

Particle tokenizer

Encode local interactions.

Topology-guided, particle-particle, particle-boundary, and particle-self branches turn the current state into particle tokens.

Super-token encoder / decoder

Compress globally. Decode locally.

Hierarchical merging builds compact super tokens; particle queries then recover globally coupled, per-particle corrections.

03 / Six dynamics

Six regimes.
One architecture.

The same transformer design models cloth, elastic solids, granular sand, Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids, and molecular dynamics.
