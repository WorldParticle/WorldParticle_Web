Pipeline

Particle Tokenizer

The particle tokenizer encodes local topology-guided, particle-particle, and particle-boundary interactions.

Super Token Encoder/Decoder

The super-token encoder hierarchically merges particle tokens to build super tokens. The super-token decoder recovers particle-level motion through a globally coupled attention mechanism.

Different Dynamics.
Unified Architecture.

The same transformer design models cloth, elastic solids, granular sand, Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids, and molecular dynamics.

Unseen initial geometry

Example sequences show Newtonian fluids flowing with varying viscosity and obstacle placements in a container; cloth colliding with a sphere; granular sand collapsing with varied initial shapes and friction; non-Newtonian fluids with varying rheological properties on different slopes; and elastic solids with varying initial rotations on a fixed slope. All test sequences use unseen configurations.

Unseen Actuation

Cloth-twisting sequences use actuation strengths not observed during training. WorldParticle remains stable for 250 frames, beyond the 200-frame training horizon.

Generalization Ability

The closest training example and an unseen test sequence are compared. Orange boxes highlight differences in fluid volume and container geometry.

More Possibilities

Molecular World Model

WorldParticle predicts protein side-chain rotational dynamics from different initial conformations at 50 fs time steps, 100× larger than the 0.5 fs steps used in traditional simulations.

Real-World Simulators

WorldParticle directly learns predictive dynamics from real-world data.
